ICS and NIMS

The ICS/NIMS documents cover FEMA's IS-100 and IS-700 courses, covering the Incident Command System (ICS) and National Incident Management System (NIMS), respectively. ICS and NIMS are used by governments ranging from local to tribal and federal (as well as a number of non-governmental organizations that work with governments) as a framework for handling some sort of incident. They provide a general structure and shared vocabulary to ensure that all parties involved in the incident response can work together efficiently to resolve the issue.

There are three components to this material:

  1. ICS and NIMS: an HTML slide deck covering the key points of the two courses
  2. IS-100 Introduction to the Incident Command System (PDF): a more thorough set of notes covering IS-100
  3. IS-700 Introduction to the National Incident Management System (PDF): a more thorough set of notes covering IS-700
